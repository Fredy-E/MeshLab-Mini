Experimental concept prototype — idea exploration only. Not a finished product and not production-ready. A small procedural explorer, not a modeling suite.

Geometry controls

Every change rebuilds the mesh from formulas and updates the statistics below the viewport.

Sphere radius 1 · torus outer radius about 1.34 · cylinder height 2, radius 1.

Defaults: 40 segments, 20 rings — 861 vertices and 1600 triangles on the sphere.

View modes

Normal colors paint the surface by normal direction; this is a shading mode, not a vector overlay.

Export

Downloads sphere.obj, torus.obj, or cylinder.obj with vertices, normals, and v//vn faces. File import, UVs, and topology editing are not implemented.

Viewport

Drag to rotate. Use the mouse wheel to zoom. Geometry is generated locally.

Drag to rotate, wheel to zoom. Without WebGL2 the canvas hides but generation and export keep working.

Limits and fallback

  • Three parametric surfaces only: sphere, torus, capped cylinder. No file import, no UVs, no topology editing.
  • Normal colors use object-space normals as a shading mode; a real normal-vector overlay remains future work.
  • Sphere poles and seams keep the classic UV-sphere layout (duplicated seam vertices, degenerate pole rows): fine for display and export, not optimized for simulation.
  • Viewing requires WebGL2. Without it the message above reports the failure, the canvas hides, and the mesh still generates and exports.
  • Fully offline: no downloaded models, no dependencies, no network requests.
